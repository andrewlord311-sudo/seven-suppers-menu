The recipes

Monday — Greek turkey meatballs in tomato orzo

Serves 435 minutes560 kcal · 36g protein · 8g fat per servingOne ovenproof pan
From this week's shop
  • Turkey breast mince (2% fat) 500g
  • Feta 100g
  • Pepper, chopped 1
  • Baby spinach (for salad) half bag
  • Cucumber + lemon juice (salad) 1 + ½
From your cupboard
  • Orzo 300g
  • Chopped tomatoes 2 tins
  • Garlic, oregano, olive oil —
  • Chicken stock cube 1
  • Egg (binds meatballs) 1
Method
  1. Oven to 200°C fan. Mix mince, egg, half the oregano, a grated garlic clove and plenty of pepper; roll into 16 balls. Kids are good at this bit.
  2. Brown the meatballs in a little oil in an ovenproof pan, 3–4 minutes. Out they come.
  3. Soften the chopped pepper and remaining garlic, then add tomatoes, orzo, stock (400ml) and the rest of the oregano. Stir well.
  4. Meatballs back on top, crumble over the feta, lid or foil on, into the oven for 15 minutes until the orzo is tender.
  5. Serve with a spinach and cucumber salad, lemon squeezed over.
Why it's here: turkey breast mince is one of the best protein-per-pound, lowest-fat mains in the shop — this is the week's health-target anchor.

Tuesday — Chickpea & spinach coconut curry

Serves 425 minutes530 kcal · 19g protein · high fibreOne pan + rice pot
From this week's shop
  • Onion, chopped 1
  • Ginger, grated thumb
  • Baby spinach half bag
From your cupboard
  • Chickpeas 2 tins
  • Light coconut milk 1 tin
  • Chopped tomatoes 1 tin
  • Curry powder + garam masala 1 tbsp + 1 tsp
  • Garlic 2 cloves
  • Basmati rice 300g
Method
  1. Rice on first. Soften the onion in oil, 5 minutes; add garlic, ginger and the spices for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Tip in tomatoes, coconut milk and drained chickpeas. Simmer 12 minutes until it thickens.
  3. Crush a few chickpeas against the pan to body up the sauce — the free thickener.
  4. Stir the spinach through in handfuls until just wilted. Season properly.
  5. Serve over rice. Chilli flakes on the table for whoever wants heat.
Cook's note: cook 600g of rice tonight, cool half fast and refrigerate — Thursday's chilli rice is then already done.

Wednesday — Baked salmon, new potatoes, peas & broccoli

Serves 430 minutes650 kcal · 34g protein · omega-3One tray + one pot
From this week's shop
  • Frozen salmon portions (defrosted overnight) 4
  • New potatoes, halved 1kg
  • Broccoli, in florets 1 head
  • Lemon 1
From your cupboard
  • Frozen peas 300g
  • Olive oil, honey, soy sauce glaze
  • Butter knob
Method
  1. Oven to 200°C fan. Toss potatoes in oil and salt; roast 15 minutes before the fish goes in.
  2. Mix 1 tsp honey with 1 tbsp soy. Sit the salmon on the tray with the potatoes, brush with the glaze.
  3. Back in the oven for 12–14 minutes until the salmon flakes.
  4. Meanwhile boil or steam broccoli and peas, 4 minutes; toss with the butter.
  5. Lemon wedges over everything at the table.
Remember tonight: move the whole chicken from freezer aisle plans to reality — and take Thursday's mince out of the freezer if you froze it.

Thursday — Hidden-veg beef chilli with rice

Serves 440 minutes520 kcal · 42g protein · 11g fat per servingOne pan
From this week's shop
  • Beef mince, 5% fat 500g
  • Onion, chopped 1
  • Carrots, grated 2
  • Greek yoghurt, to top 4 tbsp
From your cupboard
  • Kidney beans 1 tin
  • Chopped tomatoes 1 tin
  • Tomato purée 1 tbsp
  • Cumin, smoked paprika, mild chilli powder 1 tsp each
  • Rice (Tuesday's batch) for 4
Method
  1. Brown the mince hard in a dry pan — 5% fat needs no added oil. Push aside; soften the onion in the same pan.
  2. Grated carrot, garlic and spices in for 2 minutes. The carrot melts into invisibility, promise.
  3. Tomatoes, purée, drained beans and a splash of water. Simmer gently 20 minutes.
  4. Reheat Tuesday's rice until piping hot.
  5. Bowls: rice, chilli, a spoon of yoghurt. Grated cheddar for those who've earned it.
Health swap: yoghurt instead of soured cream saves ~9g fat per serving and nobody has ever noticed.

Friday — Chicken fajitas

Serves 425 minutes700 kcal · 39g proteinOne pan, table service
From this week's shop
  • Chicken breast, in strips 650g
  • Peppers, sliced 2
  • Tortilla wraps 8
  • Greek yoghurt rest of pot
  • Lime 1
From your cupboard
  • Fajita seasoning 2 tbsp
  • Cheddar, grated 75g
  • Onion 1
Method
  1. Toss the chicken strips in the seasoning. Get the pan properly hot.
  2. Sear the chicken in two batches so it browns rather than stews — 4 minutes each. Set aside.
  3. Same pan: peppers and onion until charred at the edges. Chicken back in, squeeze of lime.
  4. Warm the wraps in a dry pan or the toaster.
  5. Everything to the table in dishes — building your own is the whole point of Friday.
Current champion: this slot is reserved for the family favourite until the votes crown a new one.

Saturday — Sausage, butter bean & tomato one-pot

Serves 445 minutes590 kcal · 29g proteinOne pot · this week's new recipe
From this week's shop
  • Reduced-fat pork sausages 8
  • Onion, sliced 1
  • Crusty baguette 1
From your cupboard
  • Butter beans 2 tins
  • Chopped tomatoes 1 tin
  • Frozen spinach 3 blocks
  • Smoked paprika, oregano, garlic —
  • Chicken stock cube 1
Method
  1. Brown the sausages all over in a casserole, 8 minutes. Out; slice each into three chunks.
  2. Soften the onion in the sausage fat; garlic, paprika and oregano in for a minute.
  3. Tomatoes, 200ml stock, drained beans, spinach blocks and the sausages back in.
  4. Simmer with the lid ajar for 25 minutes until thick and glossy.
  5. Big bowls, torn baguette, and everyone mops.
New recipe: Saturday's vote counts double this week — it decides whether this earns a place in the rotation.

Sunday — Roast chicken with all the trimmings

Serves 4 tonight + seeds 2 meals next week1 h 40 (25 min hands-on)750 kcal · 40g protein
From this week's shop
  • Whole chicken ~1.6kg
  • White potatoes 1.5kg
  • Carrots rest of bag
  • Spring greens 1 head
  • Lemon (in the cavity) ½
From your cupboard
  • Olive oil, flour (gravy) —
  • Chicken stock cube 1
  • Bay leaves, garlic —
Method
  1. Oven to 190°C fan. Lemon half and a garlic clove in the cavity; oil, salt, pepper on the skin. Roast ~1h20 (until juices run clear).
  2. Parboil potatoes 8 minutes, shake the pan hard to roughen, then roast in hot oil for the final hour, turning once.
  3. Carrots in with the potatoes for the last 35 minutes. Greens: 4 minutes in boiling water right before serving.
  4. Rest the bird 15 minutes; make gravy from the pan juices, flour and stock.
  5. After dinner — the ten minutes that pays for itself: strip every scrap of meat into a tub (fried rice, Monday). Carcass, bay, an onion half into a pot of water; simmer while you clean up, then strain and refrigerate — next week's curry and gravy stock.
Cook once, eat three times: tonight's £4.10 bird is also next Monday's dinner and a litre of proper stock.
